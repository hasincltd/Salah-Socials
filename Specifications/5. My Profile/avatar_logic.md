# My Avatar Logic

## Access
- First swipeable tab in My Profile
- Replaces Posts/Stories area (no Instagram-style posts)

## Customisation Categories
- Appearance (skin tone, facial features)
- Hairstyles
- Outfit (tops, bottoms, full outfits)
- Shoes
- Accessories (glasses, hats, bags)

## Lock/Unlock System
- Locked items shown at 25% opacity with gold padlock overlay and SS Coin price
- Unlocked via SS Coins
- Seasonal exclusive items only available during their season

## Display Locations
- My Profile screen
- Full Profile popup (self and friends)
- Leaderboard full profile view
