# Salah Seasons

## Structure
- Season duration: 30 days
- Themed Islamic name e.g. Ramadan Season, Dhul Hijjah Season, Muharram Season
- 10 milestone rewards on a linear progress bar
- Progress = Streak Score accumulated during season period only
- Season ends → all unclaimed rewards locked forever
- New season starts automatically

## Reward Types
- Avatar items (clothing, hairstyles, accessories)
- Profile frames and borders
- Badges (season-exclusive)
- Banners
- SS Coin bonuses

## Tiers
- **Core tier:** standard rewards at each milestone
- **Premium tier:** parallel gold track with premium rewards at same milestones
- Premium users claim both tiers simultaneously
- Premium items shown at 25% opacity and locked for Core users
- Tapping locked Premium item → subscription upsell popup

## Display (My Profile — Salah Seasons tab)
- Linear progress bar showing current Streak Score vs next milestone
- Current season name and days remaining shown at top
- All 10 milestones visible with reward previews
- Claimed rewards shown as unlocked
- Unclaimed future rewards shown as locked until milestone reached
