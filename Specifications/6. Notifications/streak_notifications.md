# Streak Notifications

## Milestone Reached
- "You're on a [X] day streak 🔥 Keep going!"
- Fires at: 7, 14, 30, 100, 365 days

## Streak Lost
- "Your streak has been reset. Revive it within 24 hours? ⏰"
- Links to Revive popup

## Revive Reminder (while revive window open)
- Fires every time app is opened within 24 hour window
- "You have [X] hours left to revive your [N] day streak"

## Revive Expired
- "Your streak revive has expired. Start fresh today 💪"

## Community Streak Notifications
- "[Friend] just hit a [X] day streak 🔥"
- "[Friend] earned the [Badge Name] badge 🏅"
- "[Friend] joined [Event] at [Mosque] 🕌"
- "[Friend] is at [Mosque] — they just prayed [Prayer] there"
